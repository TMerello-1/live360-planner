H10 Build Multimodal Agents to Talk with your Data & Take Actions using Azure SQL, Cosmos DB, Azure AI

09/17/2026

11:15am - 12:30pm

Level: Intermediate

Prashant G Bhoyar

AI Architect in the Office of the CTO

AIS

AI agents are moving beyond chat into autonomous systems that reason, orchestrate workflows, and act across enterprise environments. Multimodal agents take this further by operating seamlessly across text, images, audio, and video.

In this focused, demo-driven session, Microsoft MVP and MCT Prashant G Bhoyar will walk Enterprise Architects and Senior Developers through the architecture and implementation patterns for building production-ready multimodal agents using Azure SQL, Cosmos DB, Microsoft Foundry, and Azure AI.

You will gain practical guidance on scalable design, secure integrations, and enterprise-grade governance to move from proof of concept to real-world deployment.

You will learn:

  • About Microsoft Foundry
  • About building enterprise-grade multimodal agents integrated with Azure SQL, Cosmos DB, Microsoft Foundry, and Azure AI
  • About architecture patterns, scalability considerations, and best practices
