Cutting-Edge AI

H18 Multi-Agent AI Systems with Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, Fabric, Microsoft Agent Framework

09/17/2026

3:15pm - 4:30pm

Level: Advanced

Prashant G Bhoyar

AI Architect in the Office of the CTO

AIS

Agents are the new apps for an AI-powered world where personal agents, business-process agents, and cross-organizational agents work together to enhance productivity and collaboration. With their evolution, real innovation and results are coming to fruition, and businesses are seeing real benefits.

In this session, we will cover the following topics in detail:

  • What are AI Agents?
  • What are Autonomous Agents?
  • What are Multi-Agent AI Systems?
  • How to evaluate a use case and find the ROI?
    • Enterprise-Level Applications, AI Agents: Learn to develop robust, scalable multimodal applications and AI Agents using Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Fabric, Microsoft Agent Framework, focusing on industry requirements and best practices

You will learn:

  • How to differentiate between AI agents, autonomous agents, and multi-agent systems, and understand how they transform enterprise applications.
  • About enterprise architecture patterns and best practices for building scalable, multimodal AI agents using Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Fabric, and the Microsoft Agent Framework.
  • How to evaluate AI agent use cases, identify high-value opportunities, and assess ROI in real-world business scenarios.
