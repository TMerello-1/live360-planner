For over 30 years, VSLive!® Developer Conference has been the go-to event for developers, software architects, engineers, and designers looking to stay ahead of the curve. As technology continues to evolve at an unprecedented pace, we invite you to join us in San Diego this September to expand your expertise, fuel your innovation, and take your career to the next level.

Bahia Resort Hotel,
San Diego, CA

Building Future-Ready Skills for Modern Developers

Join us for five days of immersive developer training at Visual Studio Live! (VSLive!®) San Diego 2026, the premier conference for professionals building modern applications on the Microsoft platform. Connect with Microsoft engineers, MVPs, and industry experts while exploring the latest advancements in modern .NET, ASP.NET Core, Azure, AI-powered development, GitHub Copilot, Blazor, .NET MAUI, Kubernetes, and modern data platforms.

Through hands-on labs, deep-dive workshops, and practical technical sessions, you’ll learn how to build intelligent applications, scalable cloud services, and modern web experiences while improving productivity with today’s most powerful developer tools. Join fellow developers in sunny San Diego and gain the real-world knowledge you need to stay ahead in the rapidly evolving Microsoft development ecosystem.
